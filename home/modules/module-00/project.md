# 🎯 Your Mission

Inspect a previously unseen public dataset and create **Data Literacy Field Notes** documenting what you discover.

Your work should provide evidence that you can:

* Inspect unfamiliar data.
* Classify what the data represents.
* Distinguish Structured from Unstructured Data.
* Raise a specific Data Quality question.
* Document your reasoning.
* Preserve your work with Git.
* Publish your work to GitHub.

This isn't about producing a sophisticated analysis yet.

It's about showing that you can **inspect before assuming, document your reasoning, and preserve your work professionally**.

---

## 📦 Your Deliverables

Create a new **public GitHub repository** for your project.

Your repository should include:

### 1. Data Literacy Field Notes

Create a mini-notebook documenting your inspection of the dataset.

For the columns you inspect, record:

* **Column Name**
* **Example Value**
* **Inferred Data Type**
* **What You Think the Variable Represents**

Your classifications should be based on what the data **means**, not only what it looks like.

---

### 2. Data Structure Classification

Identify whether the dataset is:

* **Structured**
* **Unstructured**

Explain what evidence supports your classification.

---

### 3. Data Quality Question

Write **one specific question** about the quality of the data.

You might notice something that makes you wonder about missing values, inconsistent formatting, unusual values, duplicate records, or how a field was collected.

Your job is to identify a question worth investigating—not necessarily solve it yet.

---

### 4. README

Include a `README.md` that helps someone understand:

* **What Dataset You Inspected**
* **What Your Field Notes Contain**
* **One Important Thing You Noticed**

---

### 5. Git History

Your repository must contain **at least two descriptive Commits**.

Avoid Commit Messages such as:

```text
update
stuff
final
```

Prefer messages that describe what changed:

```text
add initial field notes
document dataset column classifications
add data quality question
```

Your Commit history is part of your project evidence.

# Start Here

## Welcome to the Data Analytics Fellowship GitBook

This GitBook is your **mission control** for the Data Analytics Fellowship.

You'll use it to understand what you're working toward, launch required interactive readings, check what you can retrieve, and connect your technical skills to the work of a data professional.

The GitBook isn't your textbook.

Your **interactive readings are the Main Missions** where you'll build new knowledge. You'll return here to retrieve, apply, explain, and connect what you've learned.

---

## 🧭 How Learning Works Here

Most quests follow a familiar rhythm:

**Mission → Vocabulary → Warm-Up → Required Reading → Checkpoint → Interview Arena → Next Unlock**

You won't see every element on every page, but the pattern should become familiar.

### 🎯 Main Mission — REQUIRED

When you see this label, you're expected to complete the linked interactive reading.

The reading will open separately so you can return to your quest when you're finished.

### ⚡ Checkpoints

Checkpoints are low-stakes opportunities to test what you can retrieve **without immediately returning to the reading**.

Getting something wrong is useful information.

You'll usually get:

**First Attempt → Hint → Second Attempt → Answer + Explanation**

### 💼 Interview Arena

You'll regularly practice explaining technical ideas clearly.

These questions help you build the vocabulary and explanations you'll need when talking about your work with instructors, teammates, and eventually interviewers.

### 🏆 Boss Battles

Boss Battles are larger challenges where you use several skills together.

They're designed to produce stronger evidence of what you can actually do—not just what you can remember.

---

## 🎮 About the Game Language

You'll see terms such as:

**Quest · Main Mission · Checkpoint · Next Unlock · Boss Battle · Portfolio Unlock**

They're here to make your progress easier to recognize and the learning experience more engaging.

They don't represent automatic XP, lives, or locked levels.

**You control your progress.**

Use the Final Checks and Checkpoints to decide whether you're ready to move forward or whether a skill needs another round.

---

## 🎯 Keep the North Star in Sight

The goal isn't just to finish pages.

You're building toward being able to:

**Perform the Skill → Explain the Skill → Verify Your Work → Show Evidence of the Skill**

Those habits matter in projects, technical conversations, and data-role interviews.

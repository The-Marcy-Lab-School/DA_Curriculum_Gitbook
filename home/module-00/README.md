# Module 0: Introduction

## Foundations: Data Literacy, Environment & Version Control

### 🎯 Mission: Build Your Analyst Workstation

Every analyst needs a foundation they can trust.

In this module, you'll learn how to **inspect unfamiliar data, navigate your computer from the Terminal, preserve your work with Git, and verify information before trusting it**.

These aren't side skills.

They're part of how professional analysts work.

---

## 🧭 Your Quest Map

You'll complete four quests before taking on the Module 0 Checkpoint and final Boss Battle.

### Quest 0.1 — What Kind of Data Is This?

Learn to distinguish Variables, Values, Data Types, and Data Structures—and why something that looks numeric isn't always quantitative.

### Quest 0.2 — Meet Your Terminal

Begin navigating your computer through the Terminal using commands such as `cd` and `ls`.

### Quest 0.3 — Save the Story of Your Work

Use Git and descriptive Commits to preserve the history of how your project develops.

### Quest 0.4 — Confidence Is Not Evidence

Build the habit of verifying AI-generated claims before relying on them.

---

## 🛠️ Skills You'll Build

* **Data Literacy**
* **Terminal / Command Line**

* **AI Literacy**
* **Critical Thinking**
* **Curiosity**

---

## 🏆 Your Final Mission

At the end of Module 0, you'll inspect a **previously unseen public dataset** and create your **Data Literacy Field Notes**.

You'll document what you discover and publish your work through Git and GitHub.

Your final work should show more than whether you reached an answer.

It should provide evidence of **how you think, verify, document, and work**.
